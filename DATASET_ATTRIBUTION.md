# Dataset attribution

The 100-record cloud demonstration subset was derived from CICIDS2017, provided by the Canadian
Institute for Cybersecurity at the University of New Brunswick.

Official dataset page: https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/ids-2017.html

The provider states that its datasets may be redistributed, republished and mirrored when the
dataset and the research paper listed on the dataset webpage are cited.

I. Sharafaldin, A. H. Lashkari, and A. A. Ghorbani, “Toward Generating a New Intrusion Detection
Dataset and Intrusion Traffic Characterization,” in *Proceedings of the 4th International
Conference on Information Systems Security and Privacy (ICISSP)*, Portugal, 2018.

The staged subset is supplied only for demonstrating the frozen models. It is not an independent
evaluation dataset and must not be used to claim new performance results.
